Our story

A little casita of sweetness, delivered to your door.

Casita Dolce began with a simple idea: the snack table shouldn't be an afterthought — it should be a moment. We build beautifully styled snack carts that turn a corner of your event into something guests gather around, photograph, and remember.

Every cart is dressed to match your palette and your occasion, staffed with care, and served with the warmth of a home. We handle the setup, the styling, and the sweet details — you enjoy the party.
