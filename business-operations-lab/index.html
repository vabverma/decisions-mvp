Meet Giselle

From Chemist to COO — turning systems into success

Giselle's academic journey began with a Bachelor's in Chemistry followed by a Master's in Biochemistry, both from Georgia State University. Her scientific background built an analytical mindset and attention to detail that have proven invaluable throughout her career.

She honed her craft as a Chemist at The Coca-Cola Company in Atlanta, then transitioned to the legal sector — developing operational frameworks that earned her the role of Chief Operating Officer, which she held for ten years.

Today, through Business Operations Lab, Giselle partners with law firms and service-based businesses to strengthen their operations — from onboarding and documentation to performance management and process optimization. Her mission is simple: to help business owners reclaim their time, elevate their teams, and operate with confidence.
